# Privacy Policy — Driver Surfer: Traffic Rush 3D

**Last Updated:** October 27, 2026

## Overview
**Driver Surfer: Traffic Rush 3D** ("the App") is an offline 3D arcade driving game developed by Louis Basseto. We respect your privacy and are committed to complete transparency.

## Data Collection and Use
**We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, device identifiers, usage analytics, or location information.**

- **No Personal Information:** The App does not request or access your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.
- **No Analytics or Tracking:** The App contains no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising trackers, or telemetry.
- **Local Storage Only:** Your high score ("BEST" score) is saved locally on your device using standard browser/WebView local storage solely to display your personal record inside the game. This data never leaves your device.
- **100% Offline Operation:** The App runs entirely on your device and makes no external network requests.

## Children's Privacy
Because the App collects no personal information or device data of any kind from any user, it is safe for players of all ages (Rated 4+) and complies with COPPA and GDPR requirements.
